Exchange Policy

7-din mein hassle-free size exchange — koi sawaal nahi, koi condition nahi.

Our Promise to You

RAWAN Footwear mein hum jaante hain ke size selection online mushkil ho sakta hai. Isliye hum 7-day easy size exchange policy offer karte hain. Aapko koi reason nahi dena — agar size fit nahi toh hum exchange karein ge.

Exchange Eligibility

  • Delivery ke 7 calendar days ke andar exchange request karein
  • Product unworn aur original condition mein hona chahiye
  • Original packaging ke sath bhejein (box, tissue, etc.)
  • Leather sole par koi scratches ya wear nahi honi chahiye
  • Size exchange sirf available sizes ke liye possible hai

How to Exchange — 3 Simple Steps

📱
Step 1 — WhatsApp
Apna Order ID aur required size WhatsApp par send karein: 03xx-xxxxxxx
📦
Step 2 — Ship Back
Hum aapko return address dein ge. Product securely pack karke courier karein.
🎉
Step 3 — Receive New Size
Product receive hone ke baad 2 business days mein naya size dispatch ho jata hai.

Shipping Cost for Exchange

  • First Exchange: Return shipping ka kharcha customer ka, forward shipping RAWAN ki taraf se free
  • Second Exchange onwards: Dono taraf ka shipping customer ka
  • Our Error (Wrong item/size): Poora shipping free — hum responsible hain

Refund Policy

Hum filhal size exchange offer karte hain — cash refund available nahi hai. Agar product defective aayi toh hum full exchange ya store credit denge.

Defective product ke liye 24 hours ke andar WhatsApp par photos send karein.

What Cannot Be Exchanged

  • Worn shoes (sole wear visible ho)
  • Damaged products (customer ki taraf se)
  • 7 din ke baad requests
  • Sale / Clearance items (clearly marked hote hain)

Exchange Karna Ho?

Seedha WhatsApp par contact karein — hum jald se jald response dein ge.
