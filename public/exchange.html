Exchange Policy

Easy size exchange within 7 days. No questions asked, no conditions.

Our Promise to You

At RAWAN Footwear, we know that choosing a size online can be hard. That is why we offer an easy 7-day size exchange. You do not need to give a reason. If the size does not fit, we will exchange it.

Exchange Eligibility

  • Ask for an exchange within 7 calendar days of delivery
  • The product must be unworn and in original condition
  • Send it back in the original packaging (box, tissue, etc.)
  • The leather sole must have no scratches or wear
  • Size exchange is only possible for sizes that are in stock

How to Exchange — 3 Simple Steps

📱
Step 1 — WhatsApp
Send your Order ID and the size you need on WhatsApp: 03xx-xxxxxxx
📦
Step 2 — Ship Back
We will give you the return address. Pack the product safely and send it by courier.
🎉
Step 3 — Receive New Size
We send the new size within 2 business days after we receive the product.

Shipping Cost for Exchange

  • First Exchange: The customer pays return shipping. RAWAN pays shipping to you for free
  • Second Exchange onwards: The customer pays shipping both ways
  • Our Error (Wrong item/size): All shipping is free. We are responsible

Refund Policy

For now we offer size exchange. Cash refunds are not available. If the product arrives defective, we will give a full exchange or store credit.

For a defective product, send photos on WhatsApp within 24 hours.

What Cannot Be Exchanged

  • Worn shoes (if sole wear is visible)
  • Products damaged by the customer
  • Requests made after 7 days
  • Sale / Clearance items (these are clearly marked)

Want to Exchange?

Contact us directly on WhatsApp. We will reply as soon as we can.
