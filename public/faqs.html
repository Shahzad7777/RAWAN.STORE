Frequently Asked Questions

Sawalon ke jawab — quick aur clear.

Sizing & Fit
Agar aap normally EU 42 pehante hain toh EU 42 hi lein. Hamari sizing standard European hai. Har product page par fit indicator (True to Size / Runs Large) clearly mention hai — wo zaroor check karein. Raah Runner ke liye specifically size down karna recommend karte hain.
Hum EU sizing use karte hain jo UK/US se thodi alag hai. EU 42 = UK 8 = US 8.5. Apne paon ki length CM mein naap ke size guide check karein — product page par available hai.
Koi fikar nahi — 7-day easy exchange available hai. WhatsApp par message karein aur hum exchange process guide karein ge. Shoes unworn honi chahiyein.
Delivery & Shipping
Major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad) mein 2–3 business days. Other cities mein 3–5 business days. Remote areas (Balochistan, FATA) mein 5–7 days. Orders placed before 2 PM same day dispatch hote hain.
Rs. 4,999 ya us se upar ke orders par free delivery milti hai. Us se kam orders par Rs. 250 delivery charge lagta hai.
Ji haan — Cash on Delivery poore Pakistan mein available hai. Remote areas (Quetta, Gwadar, Gilgit, etc.) mein Rs. 200 ka refundable advance deposit required hota hai jo delivery par adjust ho jaata hai.
TCS, Leopards Courier, M&P, PostEx aur Trax use karte hain — location ke hisaab se best courier select kiya jaata hai. Dispatch hone ke baad tracking number WhatsApp par milta hai.
Orders & Payment
Order place hone ke baad aapko WhatsApp par verification message milega. Hum order details confirm karein ge aur COD orders ke liye verbal confirmation lenge. Phir packing aur dispatch hoga.
Ji haan — advance bank transfer par 5% discount milti hai. Checkout par "Bank Transfer" select karein aur payment screenshot WhatsApp par bhejein.
Dispatch se pehle cancellation possible hai — WhatsApp par contact karein. Agar order dispatch ho gaya toh delivery reject na karein — exchange process use karein.
Product Quality
Jo products leather hain wo genuine full-grain leather hain — koi artificial/PU leather nahi. Product description mein clearly mention hai. Suede products real suede hain jo specially treated hai.
Proper care ke sath leather shoes 3–5+ saal aur canvas shoes 2–3 saal chalte hain. Care guide follow karein jo har product page par diya hua hai. Leather ko monthly condition karna important hai.
