The Craft

Every Shoe Is a Promise.

We use only full-grain and top-grain leather. We never use cheap bonded leather. Suede is specially treated to resist moisture.
Premium styles use Goodyear welt construction, so they can be resoled. Lightweight styles use Blake stitch. We never use glue-only construction.
Rubber-blend outsoles tested on many kinds of ground in Pakistan, from smooth marble floors to rough outdoor surfaces.
Every pair is checked by hand before dispatch. We check three things: stitching, sole glue and leather finish.
